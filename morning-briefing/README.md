# Morning Briefing

Daily market intel briefing delivered to your Telegram. No trades — pure research and analysis.

## What This Agent Does

- Delivers a structured market briefing at the same time every day
- Covers macro regime, top movers, key levels for your watchlist, and upcoming catalysts
- Optionally sends off-schedule alerts for major market events
- No trading — this is your personal crypto research analyst

## Briefing Structure

1. **Macro Regime** — Risk-on/risk-off, DXY/yields/VIX context
2. **Top Movers** — What moved and why
3. **Watchlist Levels** — Key support/resistance for your assets
4. **Upcoming Catalysts** — Events in the next 24-48 hours
5. **Bottom Line** — The single most important thing today

## Onboarding Flow

1. Set your watchlist (which assets to cover)
2. Choose delivery time
3. Pick briefing depth (quick summary, standard, or deep dive)
4. Configure alert preferences for major events
5. Receive a test briefing to confirm format

## Prerequisites

- A Gigabrain SuperAgent (daemon)
- Telegram bot token (for delivery)

## Required Skills

- `gigabrain-intel` — Market analysis and intelligence
